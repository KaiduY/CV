\cvsection{Research Interests}


%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
%	CONTENT
%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{cvparagraph}
  Mathematical physics, particularly the application of algebraic, categorical, homological and homotopical methods to theoretical physics. Specific interests include algebraic topology, homotopy theory, higher algebra, category theory, operads, Hopf algebras, quantum groups and Grothendieck--Teichmüller theory, especially their connections to quantum field theory and related mathematical structures.

  Additional interests include pure mathematics, quantum information theory and the mathematical foundations of theoretical physics.
\end{cvparagraph}
